Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

QubixNode is an independent website about AI tools and smart technology, available at gulsher-dev.github.io/qubixnode. This page explains what information is used when you visit.

Information we do not collect

You do not need an account to use QubixNode, and we do not ask for your name, email address or password.

AI features and your text

When you use the AI Chatbot, AI Tool Finder, AI Prompt Generator or AI Text Summarizer, the text you type is sent to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini) through a Cloudflare server so that a reply can be created. Please do not enter passwords, financial details or other private information into these features. AI replies can be wrong, so double-check anything important.

Reviews

Reviews you write on tool pages are saved only in your own browser on your own device. They are not sent to us or shown to other visitors.

Cookies and similar technologies

QubixNode uses your browser's local storage to remember small things, such as your saved reviews. In the future we may use analytics, advertising or affiliate services, which can use cookies to measure visits and show relevant ads. If we add them, we will update this page.

Affiliate links and advertising

Some links on QubixNode may become affiliate links in the future. This means we may earn a commission if you sign up for a tool through our link, at no extra cost to you. Our ratings and descriptions are our own opinion.

Third-party websites

QubixNode links to other websites, such as the official pages of AI tools. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites.

Children

QubixNode is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website grows. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Message us on Instagram at @qubixnode, or visit our Contact page.
